02 — About

Seven years of not stopping.

Seven years ago I programmed a LEGO robot. I haven't stopped since — I've just kept widening what counts as a robot.

I'm a 10th-grader at Green Hope High School in Cary, NC, taking dual-enrollment engineering and CS at Wake Tech and serving as president of the Society of Future Engineers. My work moves between competition robotics, shipped iOS apps, AI-powered web tools, and quantum computing experiments.

Outside of building, most of my time goes to handing the ladder back down: tutoring on Schoolhouse.world for students in nine countries, hosting the AI for Young Minds podcast, teaching robotics to middle schoolers, and founding a software development club that now trains 35 students. When I'm off the keyboard it's tennis, basketball, pickleball, or a chess tournament.

I care about craft and follow-through. One polished, working thing beats ten I never finish.
